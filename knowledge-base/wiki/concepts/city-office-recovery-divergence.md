Major office markets show divergent trajectories rather than a single national
recovery path. City-level reports in this source set indicate large differences in
vacancy, leasing momentum, transaction activity, and fiscal exposure.

San Francisco, New York City, and Washington, D.C. are useful comparative cases:
all show post-pandemic office stress, but with different mixes of stabilization,
distress concentration, and policy capacity.
