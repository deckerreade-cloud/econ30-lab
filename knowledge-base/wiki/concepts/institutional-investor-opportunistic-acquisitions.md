This concept captures how distress periods can create acquisition windows for
well-capitalized institutional buyers. In this source batch, selective recovery in
transaction activity appears alongside continued stress in weaker assets.

That combination implies asymmetric outcomes: some market participants absorb
losses while others can deploy capital into discounted properties.
