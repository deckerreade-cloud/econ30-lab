Flight to quality describes a K-shaped office recovery where top-tier assets
outperform older or less competitive inventory. Sources in this batch repeatedly
describe better relative outcomes for premium buildings versus Class B/C stock.

This mechanism reconciles apparently contradictory headlines: leasing and pricing
can improve in selective assets even while market-level vacancy and fiscal stress
remain elevated.
