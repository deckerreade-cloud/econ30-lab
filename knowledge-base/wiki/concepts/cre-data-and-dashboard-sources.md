This concept tracks reusable quantitative sources for analysis and dashboards:
vacancy, absorption, transaction volume, debt stress, and city-level activity
metrics.

Sources include market-roundup compilations and city or sector reports that can be
used to triangulate claims rather than relying on a single vendor narrative.
