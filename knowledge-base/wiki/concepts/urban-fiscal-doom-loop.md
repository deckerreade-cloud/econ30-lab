The fiscal doom-loop concept links office weakness to local government stress:
lower values and weaker downtown utilization can pressure property-tax receipts and
service budgets, which can in turn slow broader recovery.

This source set does not treat every city as equally vulnerable, but it repeatedly
connects office market weakness with municipal budget risk and long-horizon policy
trade-offs.
