This concept tracks the structural repricing of office real estate after the
pandemic and hybrid-work shift. Across sources, elevated vacancy and weaker
cash-flow expectations are associated with persistent pressure on office values.

The source set frames office weakness as uneven rather than uniform: some market
segments show troughing or stabilization signals while others remain deeply
distressed. This is why recovery narratives often conflict across reports.
