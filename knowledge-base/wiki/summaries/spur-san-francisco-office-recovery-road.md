SPUR frames San Francisco’s office downturn as a long recovery problem requiring
active policy intervention rather than passive waiting. The source is useful for
linking office-market outcomes to local business, land-use, and budget impacts.

It complements brokerage data by emphasizing governance and implementation risks.
