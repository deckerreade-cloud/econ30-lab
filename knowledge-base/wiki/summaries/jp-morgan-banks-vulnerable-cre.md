This source assesses banking vulnerability from commercial real estate exposure and
frames risks as significant but potentially concentrated rather than uniformly
systemic.

It is especially relevant for distinguishing sector-level stress (office-heavy
portfolios, refinancing pressure) from broader macro outcomes.
