This source is a broad statistical roundup across sectors, with office as the most
distressed segment in its framing. It highlights elevated vacancy, weaker demand
relative to pre-pandemic levels, and worsening stress in debt and delinquency
metrics.

The report also emphasizes heterogeneity: premium assets and some markets show
relative resilience, while lower-quality stock remains under pressure.
