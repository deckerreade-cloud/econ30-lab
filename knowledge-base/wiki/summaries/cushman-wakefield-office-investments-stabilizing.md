This report asks whether office is moving from decline into a stabilization phase.
Its value is in identifying where transaction activity and institutional capital may
be returning despite still-weak aggregate fundamentals.

The source is best used as a market-cycle signal and paired with city-level vacancy
and fiscal data for context.
